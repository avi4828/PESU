A civil aviation authority maintains a database to manage aircrafts, pilots, and flight operations.
Collections and fields.
1. aircrafts
aircraftId (String)
model (String)
manufacturer (String)
capacity (Number)
status (String) — Active / Maintenance

2. pilots
pilotId (String)
name (String)
licenseNo (String)
experienceYears (Number)
assignedAircraft (String)

3. flights
flightCode (String)
source (String)
destination (String)
departureTime (String)
aircraftId (String)
pilotId (String)

1. Insert a new aircraft with ID A320, model A320neo, manufacturer Airbus, capacity 180, and status Active.

2. Insert two pilot records with different experience levels and assigned aircrafts.

3. Add three flight records operating between different cities.

4.Display all aircraft records available in the database.

5. Retrieve all pilots who have more than 5 years of flying experience.

6. Find all flights departing from Bangalore.

7.Display details of an aircraft whose status is Maintenance.

8. Update the status of aircraft A320 to Maintenance.

9.Change the assigned aircraft of a pilot using their pilotId.

10. Modify the departure time of a specific flight.

11. Delete a pilot record using pilotId.

12. Remove all flights whose destination is Chennai.

13. Delete an aircraft record that is no longer in service.
